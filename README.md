# Privacy Policy for Packly

**Effective Date:** 14/06/2026

Packly respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Packly handles information when you use the app.

## 1. Overview

Packly is an app designed to help you create packing lists, manage items, and prepare trips. You can create items, lists, and trips, then check or uncheck items while packing so you do not forget anything important.

## 2. Information We Collect

Packly does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data from users.

The app does not require you to create an account, provide personal information, or submit any user data to use its core features.

## 3. Local Data Storage

All items, packing lists, and trips created in Packly are stored locally on your device.

This means that your data remains on your phone and is not sent to external servers, databases, cloud services, or third parties by Packly.

## 4. Use of Your Information

Because Packly does not collect any personal data, we do not use your information for analytics, advertising, profiling, marketing, or any other purpose.

## 5. Data Sharing

Packly does not share user data with third parties.

Since the app keeps your packing items, lists, and trips locally on your device, this information is not accessible to us.

## 6. Data Security

Your Packly data is stored locally on your device. The security of this data depends on the security settings of your device, such as your passcode, biometric authentication, operating system protections, and backup settings.

We recommend keeping your device secure and up to date.

## 7. Data Deletion

You can delete your items, lists, or trips directly within the app, where such functionality is available.

You may also remove all locally stored Packly data by deleting the app from your device. Please note that deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored data, depending on your device and backup settings.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Packly does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because Packly does not collect personal data, no personal information from children is stored, processed, or shared by us.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Packly does not use third-party services to collect or process user data.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this document.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
